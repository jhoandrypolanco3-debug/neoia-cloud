MIT Admissions & Research Philosophy

Why MIT? The Inseparable Bond of Mind and Hand

In FIRST Tech Challenge, a mathematical equation on paper means nothing if the robot shakes on the foam tiles, if the 12V battery drops under load, or if gear backlash causes odometric drift. The software must adapt instantly to physical reality.

This conviction—to build with your hands what you envision with your mind—is the very definition of MIT EECS (Course 6-2) and Mechanical Engineering (Course 2). My goal at MIT is to advance the frontiers of autonomous field robotics and embedded machine intelligence at MIT CSAIL and the MIT Media Lab.

• Research Focus: Autonomous Kinematics & Embedded Computer Vision
• Target Labs: MIT CSAIL & MIT Media Lab
